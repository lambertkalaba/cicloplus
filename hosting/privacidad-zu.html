Ngokusebenzisa i-CicloPlus wamukela izinqubo ezichazwe kulo mbhalo. Uma ungavumelani, sicela ungalusebenzisi uhlelo.

2. Iyiphi idatha esiyiqoqayo

Idatha ye-akhawunti. Ukuze usebenzise i-CicloPlus wakha i-akhawunti nge-imeyili nephasiwedi, ephathwa nge-Firebase Authentication (isevisi ye-Google). Sigcina ikheli lakho le-imeyili nosuku i-akhawunti eyakhiwe ngalo.

Idatha yomjikelezo neyezempilo oyifakayo. Konke okubhala ekhalendeni lansuku zonke: izinsuku zesikhathi, ukugeleza, uketshezi lomlomo wesibeletho, izinga lokushisa lomzimba eliyisisekelo, izimpawu, isimo sengqondo, isisindo, amahora okulala, amanzi owaphuzile, amanothi edayari, ucansi, izikhumbuzo zokuvimbela ukukhulelwa, ama-aphoyintimenti kadokotela, futhi uma uvula imodi yokukhulelwa, idatha ephathelene nokukhulelwa. Le yidatha yezempilo ebucayi futhi iqoqwa kuphela uma ukhetha ukuyifaka: ayikho inkambu ephoqelekile ngaphandle kwalokho okudingeka ngempela ukuze ikhalenda isebenze.

Izithombe ezinamathiselwe. Uma unamathisela isithombe kunothi yedayari, leso sithombe sigcinwa kanye nenothi.

Ukuvumelanisa ne-Health/Google Fit (ongakukhetha). Uma uvula ngesandla kuZilungiselelo ukuvumelanisa ne-Apple Health (HealthKit) noma i-Google Fit (Health Connect), uhlelo lungafunda futhi lubhale isisindo, ukulala, izinga lokushisa lomzimba nokugeleza kwesikhathi kuleyo nkundla kudivayisi yakho. Lesi sici sicishiwe ngokuzenzakalelayo futhi sivulwa kuphela ngemvume yakho ecacile; ungasicisha noma nini kuZilungiselelo noma uhoxise imvume kuzilungiselelo zezempilo zedivayisi.

Okuthandwayo kohlelo. Ulimi olukhethile, umbala wetimu, ikhodi ye-PIN (uma uvula ukukhiya nge-PIN) nokunye okuthandayo kugcinwa kudivayisi yakho kuphela.

Asiqoqi indawo, oxhumana nabo, izihlonzi zokukhangisa noma idatha yezinkundla zokuxhumana. Asibonisi izikhangiso futhi asisebenzisi amathuluzi okuhlaziya noma okulandelela izikhangiso abantu besithathu.

Ukubhalisela i-Premium (ongakukhetha). Uma ubhalisela i-CicloPlus Premium, inkokhelo iphathwa yi-Google Play (noma i-Apple App Store ku-iPhone). Asikaze sibone noma sigcine imininingwane yekhadi lakho. Ukuze sihlole ukuthi ukubhalisa kwakho kuyasebenza yini sisebenzisa i-RevenueCat, ethola umlando wokuthenga ukubhalisa nesihlonzi somsebenzisi esingaziwa.

3. Indlela idatha yakho egcinwa ngayo

Ukugcina emafwini (Firebase). Amarekhodi omjikelezo wakho agcinwa ku-Cloud Firestore, isizindalwazi se-Google Firebase, axhumene ne-akhawunti yakho (isihlonzi somsebenzisi) kuphela. Lokhu kwenza idatha yakho ihlale ikhona uma ushintsha idivayisi noma ufaka kabusha uhlelo.

Ikhophi kudivayisi yakho. Uhlelo lugcina nekhophi (cache) efonini yakho ukuze ukwazi ukubona idatha yakho ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi. Le khophi ivumelaniswa namafu ngokuzenzakalelayo lapho uxhumano lubuya.

Ukubethela. Ukuxhumana phakathi kohlelo ne-Firebase kubethelwe (HTTPS/TLS). I-Firebase ibethela idatha egcinwe kumaseva ayo ngokwamazinga e-Google Cloud.

Isipele sezilungiselelo zakho. Ukuze ungalahlekelwa yilutho uma ushintsha ifoni, sigcina nezilungiselelo zohlelo kanye nenqubekela phambili kaMichi ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Firebase. I-PIN yakho nezithombe zakho azigcinwa.

4. Sabelana ngedatha nobani

I-CicloPlus ayiyidayisi idatha yakho futhi ayabelani ngayo nabakhangisi. Sabelana ngolwazi kuphela no:

  • I-Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication ne-Cloud Firestore): umhlinzeki wethu wengqalasizinda yokungena nokugcina idatha. I-Google icubungula le datha egameni lethu, ngokwezibophezelo zayo zokuphepha nokuvikela idatha njengomcubunguli.
  • I-RevenueCat ne-Google Play / Apple App Store: kuphela uma ubhalisela i-Premium, ukuze kucutshungulwe inkokhelo futhi kuhlolwe ukubhalisa. Bathola umlando wokuthenga nesihlonzi somsebenzisi esingaziwa, hhayi neze idatha yakho yezempilo.
  • I-Apple Health / Google Health Connect: kuphela uma uvula ngokuzithandela ukuvumelanisa okuchazwe esigabeni 2, futhi kuphela ezinhlotsheni zedatha ozivumelayo.

Asabelani ngedatha yakho nezinkampani zomshwalense, abaqashi, izinkundla zokuxhumana noma noma yimuphi umuntu wesithathu ngezinjongo zokuhweba noma zokukhangisa. Ngeke sidalule idatha yakho yezempilo kubantu besithathu ngaphandle uma umthetho udinga (isibonelo, umyalo wenkantolo ovumelekile).
